# Drishti — Your Manual Pre-Demo Checklist
Do this yourself, in order, with your own hands. Don't skip steps. Check the box only when you've actually seen it work, not when you assume it does.

## Before you start
- [ ] Open the app fresh (close any old tabs, reload completely)
- [ ] Have a stopwatch or phone timer ready
- [ ] Use the actual device/browser you'll demo with

---

## 1. First screen (Role Gate)
- [ ] Click "Station" role → does it take you to Station Console? Does it look clean, nothing overlapping or cut off?
- [ ] Go back, click "Command" role → does it take you to Command Console?
- [ ] **Red flag to watch for:** any blank white flash, stuck loading spinner, or error text on screen.

## 2. Station Console
- [ ] Do you see a real list of FIR cases (not empty, not "undefined")?
- [ ] Do the numbers at the top (case counts) look like real numbers, not 0 or blank?
- [ ] Does the small map show actual pins?

## 3. Command Console — top to bottom, slowly
- [ ] **Executive summary numbers** — do they look real (not 0, not blank, not "NaN" or "undefined")?
- [ ] **Anomaly alert banner** — is there a message about a crime spike? Does it look correctly worded (not a template placeholder)?
- [ ] **Occupation chart** — do you see bars/percentages for things like "Farmer," "Business," etc.?
- [ ] **Resolution panel** — do different stations show different numbers? (If every station shows the exact same number, that's wrong — flag it.)
- [ ] **Network graph** — click on one dot (node). Does something open showing case details?
- [ ] **MO badges** (method-of-crime tags) — click one badge (e.g. "Armed Robbery"). Does a list of real cases open?
- [ ] **Generate Report button** — click it. Does a PDF actually download? Open the PDF — does it have real numbers in it, not blank?

## 4. Map screen
- [ ] Turn the "Hotspot" toggle on/off — do colored clusters appear/disappear on the map?
- [ ] Turn the "Anomaly" toggle on/off — do markers appear/disappear?
- [ ] Move the time slider — do the hotspot dots change when you move it?

## 5. Phone/mobile check
- [ ] Open the app on your actual phone (not just a resized browser window)
- [ ] Check Station Console — does everything fit, nothing cut off or overlapping?
- [ ] Check Command Console — same check.

## 6. Say it out loud, with a timer running
- [ ] Do the entire demo (steps 1–4 above) while actually SPEAKING as if judges are watching — the real words you'll say.
- [ ] Time the whole thing. Is it comfortably under your time slot? If not, decide right now what to cut.

## 7. Get a fresh pair of eyes
- [ ] Ask a teammate who hasn't been staring at this for days to click through with ZERO explanation from you.
- [ ] Watch where they pause, look confused, or ask "wait, what's this?" — that's exactly where a judge will get stuck too. Note it down.

## 8. Test on real conditions
- [ ] If possible, try it on the same wifi/network type you'll actually demo on (not your home internet).
- [ ] Time how long the first page takes to load on that connection.

## 9. Backup plan
- [ ] Confirm you have the recorded demo video/screen recording saved somewhere easy to reach (phone, laptop, both).
- [ ] If live wifi fails or something breaks mid-demo, know exactly which video you'll switch to instead of panicking.

---

## If something fails any of the above
Don't try to fix it yourself in code. Write down exactly what you saw (what you clicked, what appeared, what you expected instead) and bring it back here — I'll figure out whether it's a real bug worth fixing or something safe to just avoid clicking during the actual demo.

## If everything above passes
You're done. Stop touching code. Move fully into rehearsing what you'll say — the product itself is ready.
